Brain Sparkzz

Tiny questions, big sparks of science

Brain Sparkzz makes short, friendly science videos for curious kids aged 8 to 10. Each one answers a single how or why question about animals, nature, space, the human body, the weather or how things work.

The visuals and voices in the videos are AI-generated, and the music is made by code.
