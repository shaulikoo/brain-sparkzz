Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Brain Sparkzz YouTube channel and this website.

Made for kids

The Brain Sparkzz channel is made for kids. Every video is marked as made for kids on YouTube.

This website

This site sets no cookies and collects no data. It has no ads, no tracking and no forms.

How the channel uses YouTube

The videos are published by automation that runs on the owner's own computer. It uses YouTube API Services only for the owner's own channel: uploading videos, reading video status, changing video privacy, setting channel branding and reading YouTube Analytics reports for the channel.

That data stays on that computer and is never sold or shared.

Viewers

The channel does not collect, store or receive any personal information about viewers.

Google and YouTube

Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).

Access given to the automation can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project page at https://github.com/shaulikoo/brain-sparkzz.
